# Love Story Film Director Skill

This is a reusable instruction skill for Grok-compatible workflows.

## How to use in the regular Grok app
1. Download and unzip this package.
2. Upload `SKILL.md` into a Grok chat as a Markdown file.
3. Tell Grok: "Use the attached Love Story Film Director Skill. Turn the story below into a complete 3–4 minute, 16:9 cinematic film production package."
4. Paste the Love Story.
5. Upload reference photos of Жандос and Қарақат if available. Ask Grok to preserve their identities exactly.
6. Generate the still images and video clips shot by shot.

Uploading the file to a normal Grok chat applies its instructions to that conversation; it does not permanently install a skill in the consumer Grok app. Permanent skill support depends on the specific Grok environment (for example, Grok Build or Grok Bot).

## What it does
- Breaks the story into timed cinematic shots.
- Produces a separate still-image prompt and video prompt for each shot.
- Maintains character, wardrobe, location, and prop continuity.
- Plans motivated match cuts, sound bridges, dissolves, J-cuts and L-cuts.
- Keeps dialogue in the original language and generation prompts in English.
- Includes a privacy-respecting treatment of the phone-number plot.
